### Note on Length

The manuscript's length reflects the book's comprehensive scope: a new analytical framework (Part I), detailed empirical mapping of the investment complex (Part II), systematic analysis of three layers of financial intermediation (Part III), empirical documentation of six causal mechanisms (Part IV), application across the full global hierarchy (Part V), and systemic risk and scenario analysis (Part VI).

The author is open to discussing condensation options with the editor, including:
- Combining Parts IV and V into a single empirical section (~30,000 words saved)
- Reducing the financial intermediation section from three to two chapters (~9,000 words saved)
- Publishing the scenarios and systemic risk analysis as a separate companion policy paper

A condensed version of approximately 100,000–110,000 words is feasible without losing the core analytical contribution.
