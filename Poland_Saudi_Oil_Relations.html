What is a JDA?

A Joint Development Agreement is a framework to jointly prepare a project before a Final Investment Decision (FID). It typically covers: roles, workplan/timeline, technical & economic studies, governance, and use of results.

05 / Pillar 3

Technology & Petrochemicals — Strategic Alignment

Scale of Partnership

  • Contracted crude supply: 400 kb/d
  • ≈ 45% of PKN Orlen's crude needs
  • Group scale: 7 refineries, ~46 mtpa

Gdańsk JDA

  • Joint preparation of petrochemical project
  • Partners: Orlen + Aramco + SABIC
  • Includes a steam cracker development
What is a Steam Cracker?

A core petrochemical unit that converts hydrocarbon feedstocks (e.g., naphtha/ethane) at very high temperature with steam into key building blocks: ethylene and propylene.

Business rationale: Shifts part of the value mix from fuels toward higher-value petrochemical outputs (different margin and demand profile).

Aramco's Strategic Direction

Aramco's corporate target: expanding liquids-to-chemicals capacity to approximately 4 million b/d.

The Gdańsk petrochemical pathway aligns with Aramco's broader strategy of increasing petrochemicals' share in the value chain.

07 / Conclusions

Key Management Takeaways

1
Complete Supply Reorientation
Russia 100% → 0%, Saudi Arabia 0% → 51–60% within approximately 2–3 years. Full strategic pivot achieved.
2
Trade + Capital Integration
$1.15bn equity investment combined with offtake mechanisms (up to 50% of Gdańsk output) reinforces long-term commercial stability.
3
Relational Simplicity
Cooperation concentrated around one core partner (Aramco/SABIC) enables faster coordination, streamlined decision-making, and strategic alignment.
4
Second Growth Track
Petrochemicals (steam cracker) and liquids-to-chemicals as a natural extension beyond crude supply — higher value-added, diversified margin profile.
